## Authorized use only

This tool may only be used:

- On systems you own outright
- On systems for which you have **explicit written authorization** from the owner
- As part of a formal **penetration testing engagement** with a signed scope-of-work agreement
- Within the scope of an active **bug bounty program** that covers the target system

## What "authorized" means

Authorization must be:

- **Written** — verbal permission is not sufficient
- **Specific** — it must identify the target systems by hostname, IP range, or URL
- **Current** — authorization does not carry over from past engagements
- **From the right person** — authorization must come from the system owner, not just an employee

## Responsible disclosure

If you discover CVE-2026-41940 affecting a system during an authorized engagement:

1. Document your findings with evidence
2. Report immediately to the system owner or their security team
3. Follow coordinated disclosure timelines (typically 90 days)
4. Reference the [cPanel Security Advisory](https://support.cpanel.net/hc/en-us/articles/40073787579671-cPanel-WHM-Security-Update-04-28-2026) and patch details when reporting
